# 🎓 EduAll – Your Path to Academic Success

## 📌 Project Name

**EduAll** — A smart, all-in-one education platform that helps students find the right mentors, prepare for university applications, and unlock their full academic potential.

---

## 🎯 Goal

EduAll exists to make quality education guidance accessible to every student. Whether you're dreaming of getting into a top university, preparing for the SAT, or looking to stand out with a passion project — EduAll connects you with the right mentors and resources to make it happen.

The platform has two sides: a **student-facing website** where anyone can explore programs and sign up, and a **management dashboard** where the EduAll team keeps everything running smoothly behind the scenes.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌐 For Students & Parents

**🌍 Available in Multiple Languages**
The website automatically adapts to your preferred language, making it easy for students and families from different backgrounds to find the information they need.

**📚 Wide Range of Programs**
From getting into your dream university to acing your exams, EduAll covers it all:
- 🎓 Admissions Mentoring — for Undergraduate, Graduate, and Transfer applicants
- 📖 Academic Tutoring — personalized subject support
- 📝 SAT Preparation — structured prep to boost your scores
- 🏆 Olympiad & Skillset Tutoring — for students aiming to compete at a higher level
- 💡 Passion Project Mentoring — turn your ideas into impressive extracurriculars
- 🌱 Global Innovators Project — for students who want to make a real-world impact
- 🏫 Exclusive School Programs — tailored programs for partner schools

**📰 Blog & Learning Resources**
Stay informed with articles, guides, and tips written by education experts — all organized and easy to browse.

**🎤 Webinars & Workshops**
Join live online events hosted by mentors and education professionals to get insights and ask your burning questions.

**📖 Resource Library**
Access success stories from past students, downloadable guidebooks, video testimonials, and a student project showcase — all in one place.

**👨‍🏫 Meet Our Mentors**
Browse detailed profiles of EduAll's mentors — see their backgrounds, expertise, and what they can help you with before you even sign up.

**💼 Join the EduAll Team**
Interested in working with EduAll? Browse open job positions and apply directly through the website.

**📩 Easy Sign-Up**
Simple and dedicated sign-up pages for every program, so you always know exactly where to go to get started.

---

### 🛠️ For the EduAll Team (Admin Dashboard)

**🔐 Secure Access**
Only authorized team members can log in to the dashboard — keeping all data safe and protected.

**✍️ Content Publishing**
Easily write, edit, schedule, and publish blog posts with a built-in rich text editor. Save drafts, publish when ready, and highlight top articles.

**👥 Manage Mentors & Tutors**
Add new mentors, update their profiles, upload videos, and control who appears on the public website — all from one place.

**📊 Track Student Results**
Keep records of academic scores, SAT results, and competition achievements to showcase student success on the website.

**📅 Event & Date Management**
Post important dates, upcoming events, and webinar schedules so students are always in the loop.

**💬 Testimonials & Success Stories**
Collect and publish student testimonials and success stories to inspire the next generation of applicants.

**🏫 University & Partnership Data**
Manage a database of universities and partner institutions, keeping information accurate and up to date.

**💼 Hiring Management**
Post new job openings and review applicants for each position — all within the same dashboard.

**⚡ One-Click Maintenance**
The team can instantly refresh the website's performance with a single click, keeping everything fast and up to date.
